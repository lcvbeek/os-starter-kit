# LNO triage: how much effort does a task deserve?

Tag each task before Claude starts, and size the work to the tag.

| Tag | Meaning | Claude's job | Your job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L** Leverage | Your judgment is the value; the outcome changes something that matters | Prepare inputs, pressure-test your view | Think first, decide |
| **N** Neutral | Needs doing well enough | Draft once, no second round unless you ask | Check, send |
| **O** Overhead | Needs doing, nobody cares how | Do it whole, batch it, or propose dropping | Glance |

## Rules of thumb
- Tag by the item's outcome, not its next step. "Book a vendor session" is L if the outcome is choosing the shortlist.
- A whole live decision thread is L; forwarding inside it is O.
- Relays are O even when urgent. Urgent is not leverage.
- Expect few L items. More than a third of a list as L means some are overrated.

## Prediction first (for L items)
Before Claude shows its draft or an executive's view lands, write one line: your call plus the biggest reason. Afterwards Claude names where you differ in one line. The difference is either the learning or the pushback. Skip when you've already stated a view.

## Optional second axis: now vs steady state
If your role has a launch/growth mandate on top of day-to-day work, tag a second letter: project work vs business-as-usual. BAU is mostly N/O; that's fine, not drift.
