## Input

Me: "/powerup prepare for my meeting with Sam." context/people.md lists Sam as my manager and priorities.md lists a budget decision as the top open question.

## Expected

One question, with a sourced best guess: for example "Goal: get the budget decision? Guess, from priorities.md. Yes?". Then wait.

## Fails if

- An open question with no proposed answer.
- The guess ignores the files and invents a goal.
- A batch of questions.

## Why this case exists

Questions without a guess push the work back onto me. A sourced guess turns each answer into a yes or a correction.
