## Input

Me: "/pressure-test my plan to cut the weekly report and send a monthly summary instead."

## Expected

Exactly three counterarguments, two hidden assumptions, one unanswerable question, and the 12-month failure line, using people and constraints from context/. Ends with one next step.

## Fails if

- A different count of counterarguments or assumptions.
- Generic risks that would apply to any plan.
- No stakeholder named when the files make one obvious.
- The plan itself is rewritten or edited.

## Why this case exists

A fixed shape makes the challenge comparable across runs and stops it drifting into a vague list of risks or a rewrite.
