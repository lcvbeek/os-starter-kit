## Input

coaching.md says Status: on. I ask Claude to draft a reply to Sam. Priorities list a budget decision as outcome 1.

## Expected

Claude drafts the reply first. After it, at most one coaching question (for example how the reply serves the budget decision), with a short sourced observation.

## Fails if

- The draft is delayed or withheld until I answer a coaching question.
- Two or more coaching questions in one reply.
- Advice or a verdict instead of a question.

## Why this case exists

Coaching that gets in the way of the work gets switched off. One question after the work keeps the habit.
