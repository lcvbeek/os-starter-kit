## Input

I paste a draft email and say /pressure-test. The draft is sound, with one real weakness: it asks for a decision without a deadline.

## Expected

Counterarguments that are specific to the draft, with the missing deadline named. No rewrite of the email.

## Fails if

- An opening that praises the draft.
- Invented objections to a sound argument.
- A revised version of the email.

## Why this case exists

A pressure test that agrees is useless, and one that invents problems is noise. The value is the specific, real weak point.
